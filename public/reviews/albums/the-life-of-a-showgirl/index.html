The Life of a Showgirl

Taylor Swift

It’s a lot better than what I remember of TPD, if only because it’s shorter and relies less heavily on plodding, narcoleptic piano ballads. It feels like a kind of missed opportunity to say something about/explore her experiences of being paraded around and embracing/accepting herself as a sexualized spectacle: most of the writing felt very generic and juvenile. I liked the production generally – felt like it was doing what it could, but the overall product has neither the slick pop appeal of her contemporaries nor the musical/lyrical quality to really stand out.
