Dodi and Diana

Written by Kareem Fahmy
4/28/26 | Capital Repertory Theater

What can one say about Dodi and Diana that has not already been said about the war in Iran:

“Why are we here? How did this happen? How can we stop whoever did this from causing us further misery?”

I knew going in that this one would be a challenge, so I liquored up beforehand, but that only enhanced the utter strangeness of watching two 30-year olds hip thrust into a strobe-lit room full of elderly people to a club remix of Whitney Houston’s I Will Always Love You. I really hated this thing, but am forced to recommend it for that experience alone.
