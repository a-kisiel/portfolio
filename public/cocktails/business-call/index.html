Business Call

September 16, 2026

A cross between a Tom Collins and The Business:

  • 2oz gin
  • 1oz lime juice
  • 3/4oz honey syrup (1:1)
  • seltzer

Add the first three ingredients to a collins glass filled with ice. Top with seltzer and stir with a straw until mixed.
