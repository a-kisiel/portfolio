Handsome Chris Pullover

February 8, 2026

Made from this pattern on Ravelry. Can’t believe it’s free: very clear and easy to follow, with lots of sizing options.

For anyone that wants to try it out themselves trust their sleeve length measurements. I got to where they said to stop and said “Oh, these are too short. I’m going to keep going for another few inches.” And now I have a sweater with sleeves that are several inches too long. I am a fool, and look the part.

Also can highly recommend getting all the yarn at once from the same dye lot…subtler mistake, but the finished product has four distinct “natural” colors.
