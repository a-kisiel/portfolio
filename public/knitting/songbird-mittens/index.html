Sororal Mittens

September 13, 2026

Made mittens for my sisters for Christmas. Managed to finish everything but the thumbs in time. They were very excited for the finished product. Two Christmases later I finally finished all 4 thumbs and was able to deliver them simultaneously. And there was much rejoicing.

Sarah’s were made from this pattern, though I thought the original design was a tad crass, so I fired up Excel and made a replacement in the same style.

Kate got the Songbird Mittens – don’t recall any modifications to the original pattern.
