Made mittens for my sisters for Christmas. Managed to finish everything but the thumbs in time. They were very excited for the finished product. Two Christmases later I finished all 4 thumbs and was able to deliver them simultaneously. And there was much rejoicing.

![Bee Kind](/images/knitting/bee_kind.jpeg)

Sarah's were made from [this pattern](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/hey-bee-otch), though I thought the original design was a tad crass, so I fired up Excel and made a replacement in the same style.

![Songbird](/images/knitting/songbird.jpeg)

Kate got the [Songbird Mittens](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/songbird-mittens) -- don't recall any modifications to the original pattern.
